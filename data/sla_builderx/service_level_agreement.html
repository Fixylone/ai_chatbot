Service Level Agreement

Tool: SLA BuilderX

1. Introduction

SLA BuilderX enables creation and management of detailed Service Level Agreements tailored to enterprise services, providing IT service managers and legal teams with a comprehensive tool for SLA development and administration.

This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions under which the Service Provider will deliver services to the Customer, as detailed in subsequent sections.

For the purposes of this Agreement, the terms "Service Provider" and "Provider" may be used interchangeably, as well as "Customer" and "Client" to refer to the receiving party.

1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to ensure uninterrupted service delivery while allowing the Provider to suspend services at any time without prior notice.

This Agreement aims to establish clear responsibilities and expectations between the Service Provider and the Customer for the management and delivery of enterprise services using SLA BuilderX.

By defining service levels, performance metrics, and remedies, this Agreement seeks to promote transparency and accountability in service management.

1.2 Scope of Services

The Provider shall deliver services as described in the documentation, subject to changes at their discretion.

Services include but are not limited to configuration, monitoring, and support of enterprise applications facilitated through SLA BuilderX.

The Provider may update or modify the scope of services to adapt to evolving business needs and technological advancements, with such changes communicated as appropriate.

1.3 Definitions and Interpretations

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

  • Service Provider: The party responsible for delivering the services as specified in this Agreement using SLA BuilderX.
  • Customer: The party receiving the services under this Agreement.
  • Services: The IT service management and support activities provided by the Service Provider as outlined in Section 1.2.
  • Service Levels: The measurable performance standards and metrics agreed upon by the Service Provider and Customer.
  • Agreement: This Service Level Agreement, including all sections and appendices, governing the relationship between the Service Provider and Customer.

Interpretations of this Agreement shall be made in accordance with standard legal principles, giving effect to its intent and purpose as facilitated by SLA BuilderX.

1.4 Effective Date and Duration

This Agreement shall become effective on the date it is signed by both the Service Provider and the Customer (the "Effective Date").

Unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions set forth herein, the term of this Agreement shall continue for a period of one (1) year from the Effective Date.

Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

Any amendments to the duration or renewal provisions of this Agreement shall be documented and agreed upon through SLA BuilderX.
