Service Level Agreement

Tool: AuditLex

1. Introduction

This Service Level Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Customer and the Provider to define the terms and conditions under which the Provider will deliver services using AuditLex. AuditLex provides structured templates for audit reports and compliance evidence documentation, facilitating internal audit departments and compliance auditors in fulfilling their obligations.

This Agreement sets forth the mutual understanding of the parties regarding service levels, responsibilities, and performance standards to ensure a clear and effective partnership.

1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which the Provider will deliver services using AuditLex, ensuring compliance and audit readiness to the extent deemed appropriate by the parties.

This Agreement aims to define the roles, responsibilities, and expectations to facilitate a cooperative and effective service delivery relationship.

1.2 Scope of Services

The Provider shall deliver services utilizing AuditLex to support the Customer's internal audit and compliance functions. These services include the provision of structured templates for audit reports, compliance evidence documentation, and related advisory support as agreed upon in this Agreement.

The scope encompasses the deployment, maintenance, and updates of AuditLex tools necessary for effective audit report generation and compliance documentation. The Provider will ensure that the services are aligned with the Customer's operational requirements and regulatory obligations.

1.3 Definitions and Interpretations

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

  • AuditLex: The tool provided by the Provider that offers structured templates for audit reports and compliance evidence documentation.
  • Customer: The party receiving services under this Agreement, including use of AuditLex for audit and compliance purposes.
  • Provider: The party delivering services and granting access to AuditLex as described in this Agreement.
  • Service Levels: The performance standards and metrics applicable to the services provided through AuditLex, as detailed in Section 2.
  • Incident: Any event that disrupts or degrades the normal operation of services provided under this Agreement.

Interpretations of this Agreement shall be made in accordance with standard legal principles, with headings provided for convenience only and not affecting the interpretation of any provision.

2. Service Description and Performance Standards

This section outlines the services provided by the Provider using AuditLex and establishes the performance standards that govern the delivery of these services.

The Provider shall ensure that AuditLex is made available to the Customer to facilitate the creation of audit reports and compliance evidence documentation in accordance with the agreed Service Levels. These services include access to the AuditLex platform, regular updates, maintenance, and support as specified in this Agreement.

Performance standards shall be measured against the criteria detailed in subsequent subsections, including response times, resolution times, and system availability, to ensure consistent and reliable service delivery.

2.1 Service Description

The Provider shall deliver services through AuditLex that include the provision of structured templates for audit reports and compliance evidence documentation tailored to the Customer's requirements.

These services encompass the deployment, maintenance, and continuous enhancement of the AuditLex platform to support efficient audit and compliance processes.

AuditLex services also include user support, training materials, and updates necessary to maintain alignment with applicable regulatory standards and best practices.

2.2 Service Levels and Performance Metrics

The Provider commits to maintaining specific service levels for AuditLex to ensure reliable and efficient operation. These service levels are defined through measurable performance metrics as follows:

  • Response Times: The time elapsed from when the Customer submits a service request or incident report to when the Provider acknowledges receipt and begins initial assessment.
  • Resolution Times: The duration from initial acknowledgment to the resolution or workaround of the reported issue, aiming to restore service functionality.
  • Availability and Uptime: The percentage of time AuditLex is operational and accessible to the Customer, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

The Provider shall monitor these metrics continuously and report performance in accordance with the provisions set forth in Section 4.1.

2.2.1 Response Times

The Provider shall respond to all service requests and incident reports submitted by the Customer through AuditLex within the following timeframes based on the severity level:

  • Critical Severity: Response within 1 hour of receipt.
  • High Severity: Response within 4 hours of receipt.
  • Medium Severity: Response within 1 business day.
  • Low Severity: Response within 3 business days.

Response time is defined as the period from when the Provider receives the service request or incident report to when an initial assessment or acknowledgment is communicated to the Customer.

The Provider will utilize AuditLex's tracking and notification features to ensure timely acknowledgment and communication regarding all reported issues.

2.2.2 Resolution Times

The Provider shall resolve all incidents and service requests submitted by the Customer through AuditLex within the following timeframes, measured from the initial acknowledgment of the issue:

  • Critical Severity: Resolution or workaround within 4 hours.
  • High Severity: Resolution or workaround within 1 business day.
  • Medium Severity: Resolution or workaround within 3 business days.
  • Low Severity: Resolution or workaround within 5 business days.

Resolution time is defined as the period from initial acknowledgment to the restoration of service functionality or provision of an acceptable workaround that mitigates the impact on the Customer's operations.

The Provider will leverage AuditLex's incident tracking and communication tools to keep the Customer informed throughout the resolution process.

2.2.3 Availability and Uptime

The Provider shall ensure that AuditLex maintains a minimum availability of 99.9% uptime during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance periods.

Scheduled maintenance windows will be communicated to the Customer at least 48 hours in advance and will be planned to minimize disruption to the Customer's operations.

Availability is measured as the total time AuditLex is accessible and operational divided by the total time in the measurement period, expressed as a percentage.

The Provider will monitor system availability continuously using AuditLex's monitoring tools and will provide monthly availability reports to the Customer as part of the service monitoring and reporting obligations outlined in Section 4.1.

2.3 Service Level Targets and Commitments

The Provider commits to achieving the service level targets specified in Sections 2.2.1 through 2.2.3 to ensure the effective operation of AuditLex for the Customer.

These commitments include maintaining response and resolution times within the agreed thresholds for all reported incidents and service requests, as well as ensuring the minimum availability and uptime percentages are met or exceeded each calendar month.

In the event that service levels fall below the agreed targets, the Provider shall promptly notify the Customer, investigate the causes, and implement corrective actions to restore compliance with the service levels.

The Provider will document all service level performance and provide regular reports to the Customer in accordance with the monitoring and reporting procedures set forth in Section 4.1.

Both parties agree to cooperate in reviewing and adjusting service level targets as necessary to reflect changes in operational requirements or technological advancements, ensuring continued alignment with the Customer's needs and regulatory obligations.

Each party shall perform its obligations in good faith and cooperate to facilitate the successful operation of AuditLex as described herein.

The Provider shall ensure the continuous availability, maintenance, and support of AuditLex in accordance with the service levels defined in this Agreement.

The Provider is responsible for deploying updates, patches, and enhancements to AuditLex to maintain compliance with applicable regulatory standards and to improve functionality.

Additionally, the Provider shall deliver regular performance reports and participate in review meetings as specified in Section 4.3 to discuss service delivery and improvements.

The Provider will ensure that all personnel involved in delivering services through AuditLex are adequately trained and comply with the terms of this Agreement.

3.2 Customer Responsibilities

The Customer shall provide timely and accurate information necessary for the Provider to deliver services using AuditLex effectively.

The Customer is responsible for ensuring that its personnel using AuditLex are adequately trained and comply with the terms of this Agreement.

The Customer shall promptly report any incidents or service requests through AuditLex, providing sufficient detail to enable the Provider to assess and address the issues efficiently.

The Customer agrees to cooperate with the Provider during incident investigations and service reviews, providing access to relevant systems and personnel as reasonably required.

Additionally, the Customer shall comply with any usage policies and guidelines established for AuditLex to maintain service integrity and security.

3.3 Cooperation and Support

Both the Provider and the Customer agree to cooperate fully and provide mutual support to ensure the effective delivery and operation of services through AuditLex.

The Customer shall grant the Provider reasonable access to necessary systems, personnel, and information to facilitate incident resolution, service maintenance, and performance monitoring.

The Provider shall communicate promptly with the Customer regarding service status, incidents, and any required actions to maintain service levels.

Both parties commit to timely collaboration during review meetings, investigations, and any required escalations to address service issues or improvements.

Any support provided by the Customer to the Provider shall be conducted in a manner that preserves confidentiality and complies with applicable security policies.

4. Service Management and Monitoring

This section defines the processes and responsibilities related to the management and monitoring of services delivered through AuditLex to ensure compliance with agreed service levels.

The Provider shall implement continuous service monitoring using AuditLex's built-in tools to track performance metrics, detect incidents, and ensure adherence to service levels as detailed in Section 2.

Regular reporting on service performance, incidents, and corrective actions shall be provided to the Customer in accordance with the provisions set forth in Section 4.1.

Incident management procedures shall be established and maintained to promptly address service disruptions, as further described in Section 4.2.

Both parties shall participate in periodic review meetings to assess service performance, discuss issues, and plan improvements, as outlined in Section 4.3.

4.1 Service Monitoring and Reporting

The Provider shall utilize AuditLex's integrated monitoring capabilities to continuously track service performance metrics, including response times, resolution times, and system availability.

Monitoring data will be collected and analyzed to detect deviations from agreed Service Levels promptly, enabling proactive management of service quality.

Regular performance reports will be generated using AuditLex and provided to the Customer on a monthly basis, detailing compliance with Service Level targets and any incidents that occurred during the reporting period.

These reports shall include metrics summaries, incident descriptions, root cause analyses, and corrective actions taken to address any service deficiencies.

The Provider will maintain records of all monitoring data and reports for the duration of the Agreement and make them available to the Customer upon request for audit and review purposes.

Both parties agree to review monitoring reports during scheduled review meetings as described in Section 4.3 to ensure transparency and continuous improvement in service delivery.

4.2 Incident Management

The Provider shall establish and maintain incident management procedures utilizing AuditLex to ensure timely identification, reporting, and resolution of service incidents.

All incidents reported through AuditLex will be logged, categorized by severity, and assigned for resolution in accordance with the response and resolution times defined in Sections 2.2.1 and 2.2.2.

The Provider shall communicate with the Customer promptly regarding incident status, progress updates, and any required Customer actions through AuditLex's communication features.

Incident management processes will include root cause analysis, documentation of corrective actions, and preventive measures to minimize recurrence.

The Provider will ensure that incident records and related documentation are maintained within AuditLex for audit and review purposes throughout the term of this Agreement.

Both parties agree to cooperate fully during incident investigations and to participate in post-incident reviews to improve service quality and compliance.

4.3 Review Meetings and Performance Reviews

The Provider and the Customer shall conduct regular review meetings to evaluate service performance, discuss incidents, and identify opportunities for improvement in the delivery of services through AuditLex.

These meetings shall be scheduled at least quarterly, or more frequently as agreed upon by the parties, to ensure ongoing alignment with service level commitments and operational requirements.

During review meetings, the parties will examine performance reports generated using AuditLex, assess compliance with Service Levels, and review any outstanding issues or corrective actions.

The Provider shall present root cause analyses for any significant service disruptions and propose measures to prevent recurrence.

Both parties shall collaborate to update and refine service processes, performance metrics, and targets as necessary to reflect evolving business needs and regulatory changes.

Minutes of each review meeting shall be documented and shared with all participants, with action items tracked and followed up in subsequent meetings.

5. Compensation and Penalties

This section outlines the compensation mechanisms and penalties applicable in the event of non-compliance with the service levels established in this Agreement for services delivered through AuditLex.

The Provider agrees to provide service credits or other remedies as compensation to the Customer if the agreed Service Levels detailed in Section 2 are not met, subject to the terms and conditions specified herein.

Both parties acknowledge that compensation and penalties are intended to incentivize compliance and continuous improvement in service delivery rather than to serve as a primary source of damages.

Procedures for claiming compensation or penalties will be administered through AuditLex's reporting and documentation features to ensure transparency and accountability.

5.1 Service Credits and Remedies

In the event that the Provider fails to meet the agreed Service Levels specified in Section 2, the Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as a remedy for such non-performance.

Service credits will be calculated based on the extent and duration of the service level breach, as detailed in the compensation schedule agreed upon by the parties.

The Customer may apply service credits against future payments due under this Agreement, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

To claim service credits, the Customer must notify the Provider in writing within thirty (30) days following the end of the month in which the service level breach occurred, providing sufficient details and supporting evidence generated through AuditLex.

The Provider will review the claim and, if validated, apply the appropriate service credits within the next billing cycle.

Service credits shall be the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure by the Provider to meet the agreed Service Levels under this Agreement.

5.2 Penalties for Non-Performance

If the Provider fails to meet the agreed Service Levels as specified in Section 2, penalties may be imposed in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

Penalties shall be calculated based on the severity and duration of the non-performance, with reference to the compensation schedule mutually agreed upon by the parties.

The Provider agrees to accept such penalties as a means to encourage adherence to the Service Levels and to compensate the Customer for any inconvenience or impact caused by service deficiencies.

Any penalties assessed will be documented and communicated to the Customer through AuditLex, ensuring transparency and traceability.

The imposition of penalties does not preclude the Customer from pursuing other remedies available under this Agreement or applicable law.

Both parties agree to review penalty provisions periodically and adjust them as necessary to reflect changes in service requirements or regulatory obligations.

7.2 Data Security Measures

The Provider shall implement comprehensive data security measures to safeguard Customer data processed through AuditLex against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7.3 Data Breach Notification and Response

In the event of a data breach involving Customer data processed through AuditLex, the Provider shall promptly notify the Customer without undue delay and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

The notification will include details of the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of affected data subjects, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its adverse effects.
