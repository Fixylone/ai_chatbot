Data Processing Agreement

Tool: AuditLex

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Data Controller and the Data Processor to set forth the terms and conditions under which personal data will be processed in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

AuditLex provides structured templates for audit reports and compliance evidence documentation to assist internal audit departments and compliance auditors in fulfilling their obligations.

The Agreement establishes the roles, responsibilities, and obligations of each party to ensure the lawful and secure processing of personal data.

1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms under which the Data Processor will process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller, ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

This Agreement aims to establish clear obligations and responsibilities for both parties to protect the rights and freedoms of data subjects.

AuditLex facilitates this by providing standardized templates that support consistent and compliant documentation of audit reports and evidence related to data processing activities.

1.2 Scope of Data Processing

This Agreement governs the processing of personal data that the Data Processor undertakes on behalf of the Data Controller using AuditLex templates and tools.

The scope includes all categories of personal data and processing activities explicitly authorized by the Data Controller as documented in the relevant schedules and appendices.

Processing shall be limited to the purposes specified by the Data Controller and shall comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations.

1.3 Definitions and Interpretations

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

  • Data Controller: The natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which alone or jointly with others determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.
  • Data Processor: The natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.
  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("data subject").
  • Processing: Any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, erasure or destruction.
  • Data Subject: The identified or identifiable natural person to whom the personal data relates.

Interpretations of terms not defined herein shall be made in accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

2. Roles and Responsibilities

This section outlines the distinct roles and responsibilities of the Data Controller and the Data Processor in relation to the processing of personal data under this Agreement.

AuditLex provides the structured templates that support clear documentation and assignment of these roles to ensure compliance and accountability.

2.1 Data Controller Responsibilities

The Data Controller shall ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws and implement appropriate measures to protect personal data as deemed necessary.

The Data Controller is responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data and for providing clear instructions to the Data Processor in accordance with this Agreement.

AuditLex supports the documentation of these responsibilities through its structured templates, facilitating transparent and consistent compliance records.

2.2 Data Processor Responsibilities

The Data Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Data Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organization, unless required to do so by applicable law.

The Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as outlined in this Agreement and supported by AuditLex templates.

AuditLex facilitates the consistent documentation of processing activities and compliance measures undertaken by the Data Processor to fulfill its obligations under this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Data Controller's obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights.

The Data Processor shall notify the Data Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

2.3 Sub-Processing Arrangements

The Data Processor shall not engage any sub-processor without prior specific or general written authorization of the Data Controller.

Where the Data Processor engages a sub-processor, it shall impose data protection obligations on the sub-processor that are substantially similar to those set out in this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall remain fully liable to the Data Controller for the performance of the sub-processor's obligations.

AuditLex provides templates to document sub-processing agreements and monitor compliance with these requirements.

3. Data Processing Details

This section details the specific types of personal data processed, the processing activities conducted, and the categories of data subjects involved under this Agreement.

AuditLex provides structured templates to accurately capture and document these details, ensuring transparency and compliance with data protection requirements.

3.1 Types of Data Processed

The Data Processor shall process only the categories of personal data that are explicitly authorized by the Data Controller as specified in the relevant schedules attached to this Agreement.

Such personal data may include, but is not limited to, identification data, contact information, employment details, and any other data necessary for the fulfillment of the processing purposes outlined herein.

AuditLex templates support the detailed documentation and classification of these data types to ensure clarity and compliance with applicable data protection laws.

3.2 Processing Activities and Purposes

The Data Processor shall carry out processing activities strictly in accordance with the documented instructions provided by the Data Controller, as specified in the relevant schedules and appendices of this Agreement.

These processing activities may include collection, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, and erasure of personal data necessary to achieve the purposes authorized by the Data Controller.

The purposes of processing are limited to those explicitly defined by the Data Controller and may include, but are not limited to, administrative support, compliance monitoring, and audit reporting.

AuditLex facilitates the accurate documentation and classification of processing activities and purposes, supporting compliance with applicable data protection laws and ensuring transparency for all parties involved.

3.3 Data Subject Categories

The Data Processor shall process personal data relating only to the categories of data subjects explicitly authorized by the Data Controller as specified in the relevant schedules attached to this Agreement.

These categories may include employees, customers, suppliers, and any other individuals whose personal data is necessary for the fulfillment of the processing purposes outlined herein.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document and classify data subject categories accurately, ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws and facilitating transparent audit reporting.

4. Data Security and Confidentiality

This section outlines the obligations of both the Data Controller and the Data Processor to implement appropriate security measures and maintain the confidentiality of personal data processed under this Agreement.

AuditLex provides structured templates that assist in documenting security controls and confidentiality commitments, ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Both parties shall cooperate to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data, employing technical and organizational measures proportionate to the risks involved.

4.1 Security Measures and Controls

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of personal data processed under this Agreement.

These measures shall include, but are not limited to, access controls, encryption, pseudonymization where appropriate, regular security assessments, and secure data storage practices.

The Data Processor shall document these security measures using AuditLex templates to provide clear evidence of compliance and facilitate audit processes.

Both parties shall regularly review and update security controls to address emerging threats and vulnerabilities in accordance with applicable data protection laws and industry best practices.

4.2 Confidentiality Obligations

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall maintain the confidentiality of all personal data processed under this Agreement.

Confidential information shall not be disclosed to any unauthorized third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law or regulatory authority.

The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations, whether by contract or statutory duty.

AuditLex provides templates to document confidentiality commitments and agreements, supporting consistent and auditable compliance practices.

These confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

4.3 Incident Management and Breach Notification

The Data Processor shall establish and maintain appropriate incident management procedures to promptly detect, respond to, and mitigate any personal data breaches.

In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Processor shall notify the Data Controller without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach.

The notification to the Data Controller shall include all relevant information necessary to assess the nature and impact of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document incident reports and breach notifications, facilitating timely and compliant communication between the parties.

Both parties shall cooperate fully to manage the breach, including any required notifications to supervisory authorities and affected data subjects, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

5. Data Subject Rights and Access

This section defines the rights of data subjects concerning their personal data processed under this Agreement and outlines the procedures for exercising those rights.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document requests from data subjects and the corresponding responses, ensuring transparency and compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall cooperate to facilitate the exercise of data subject rights in a timely and compliant manner.

5.1 Data Subject Rights

Data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of their personal data as provided under applicable data protection laws.

The Data Controller shall ensure that data subjects can exercise these rights effectively and without undue delay.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller, as necessary, to facilitate the timely and compliant fulfillment of data subject requests using AuditLex templates to document such interactions.

Both parties shall maintain records of data subject requests and responses to demonstrate compliance with applicable data protection obligations.

5.2 Data Access and Portability

The Data Controller shall provide data subjects with access to their personal data upon request, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in fulfilling data access requests by providing the necessary information and documentation promptly.

Data subjects shall have the right to receive their personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format to facilitate data portability.

The Data Processor shall support the Data Controller in enabling data portability requests, ensuring that data is transferred securely and without undue delay.

AuditLex templates facilitate the documentation of data access and portability requests and the corresponding actions taken by both parties to ensure compliance and transparency.

5.3 Data Rectification and Erasure

Data subjects have the right to request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data concerning them.

The Data Controller shall ensure that such requests are addressed promptly and that the personal data is corrected or completed without undue delay.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in fulfilling data rectification requests by providing access to relevant data and implementing the necessary corrections as instructed.

Data subjects also have the right to request the erasure of their personal data when processing is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or when the processing is unlawful.

The Data Controller shall evaluate such requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws and ensure that personal data is erased or anonymized as appropriate.

The Data Processor shall support the Data Controller in executing data erasure requests, ensuring that all copies of the personal data are securely deleted from its systems and any sub-processors.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document data rectification and erasure requests and the actions taken, supporting transparency and compliance with applicable data protection obligations.

6. Data Transfers and International Data Flows

This section governs the conditions under which personal data may be transferred across national borders and the safeguards required to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document data transfer mechanisms and related compliance measures, facilitating transparent and auditable international data flows.

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall ensure that any transfer of personal data to a third country or international organization is conducted in accordance with the applicable legal requirements and with appropriate safeguards in place.

Such safeguards may include, but are not limited to, standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, or other approved transfer mechanisms recognized under relevant data protection regulations.

The parties shall maintain records of all international data transfers and the corresponding safeguards implemented, using AuditLex templates to support compliance and auditability.

6.1 Transfer Mechanisms and Safeguards

Any transfer of personal data to a third country or an international organization by the Data Processor shall be subject to appropriate transfer mechanisms and safeguards to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Such transfer mechanisms may include, but are not limited to, the use of standard contractual clauses approved by relevant supervisory authorities, binding corporate rules, or other legally recognized instruments.

The Data Processor shall implement and maintain these safeguards and document their application using AuditLex templates to provide clear evidence of compliance.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that all international data transfers are conducted securely and in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and applicable legal requirements.

6.2 Data Transfer Documentation

The Data Processor shall maintain complete and accurate records of all data transfers, but no documentation is required for transfers within the same country.

These records shall include details of the recipient, the categories of personal data transferred, the transfer mechanisms employed, and the safeguards implemented to protect the data during transfer.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document and manage data transfer records, ensuring transparency and compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that documentation related to data transfers is kept up to date and made available for audit and regulatory review as necessary.

7. Audits and Compliance

This section establishes the rights and procedures for conducting audits to verify compliance with this Agreement and applicable data protection laws.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document audit plans, findings, and compliance evidence, facilitating transparent and efficient audit processes.

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall cooperate fully during audits, providing access to relevant records, personnel, and facilities as reasonably requested.

Audit rights may be exercised periodically or in response to a suspected breach or non-compliance, subject to reasonable prior notice and confidentiality obligations.

Audit findings shall be documented using AuditLex templates and addressed promptly by the responsible party to remediate any identified issues or gaps.

7.1 Audit Rights and Procedures

The Data Controller shall have the right to conduct audits of the Data Processor's compliance with the terms of this Agreement and applicable data protection laws.

Such audits may be performed by the Data Controller or an appointed independent auditor, subject to reasonable prior notice and confidentiality obligations.

The Data Processor shall provide access to all relevant records, personnel, and facilities necessary to conduct the audit effectively.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document audit plans, procedures, findings, and any corrective actions required, supporting transparent and consistent audit processes.

Both parties shall cooperate fully to facilitate timely and efficient audits and to address any issues identified during the audit in a prompt manner.

7.2 Record Keeping and Documentation

The Data Processor shall maintain comprehensive and accurate records of all processing activities carried out on behalf of the Data Controller in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

These records shall include details such as the categories of personal data processed, the purposes of processing, data subject categories, recipients of the data, and any transfers of personal data to third countries or international organizations.

AuditLex provides structured templates to facilitate consistent and thorough documentation of processing activities and compliance measures.

Both parties shall ensure that documentation is retained for the duration required by applicable laws and is made available for inspection or audit upon reasonable request.

Proper record keeping and documentation are essential to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and relevant data protection regulations.

8. Term, Termination, and Data Return or Destruction

This section sets forth the duration of this Agreement, the conditions under which it may be terminated, and the obligations regarding the return or destruction of personal data upon termination.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document the term, termination procedures, and data disposition activities to ensure compliance and audit readiness.

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall comply with the agreed terms regarding the handling of personal data at the end of the Agreement to protect data subject rights and maintain data security.

8.1 Duration of the Agreement

The Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in effect for the initial term specified in the Term Sheet, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

Upon expiration of the initial term, the Agreement may be renewed or extended upon mutual written agreement of the Data Controller and the Data Processor.

Any renewal or extension shall be subject to the same terms and conditions set forth herein unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.

AuditLex provides templates to document the term and renewal details, supporting clear record-keeping and compliance management.

8.2 Termination Procedures

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice to the other party if the other party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice specifying the breach.

Termination may also occur immediately if required by applicable law or regulatory authority.

Upon termination, both parties shall cooperate to ensure an orderly transition and minimize any adverse effects on data subjects and ongoing processing activities.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document termination notices, reasons for termination, and related communications to support compliance and auditability.

8.3 Data Return and Destruction

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall, at the choice of the Data Controller, return all personal data processed on behalf of the Data Controller or securely destroy such data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

If returning the data is not feasible, the Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with a written certification confirming the secure destruction of all personal data and any copies thereof.

The Data Processor shall ensure that any sub-processors engaged in the processing of personal data also comply with the obligations regarding data return or destruction as set forth in this Agreement.

AuditLex provides structured templates to document the data return or destruction process, supporting transparency and compliance with audit requirements.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that the data return or destruction is completed promptly and in a manner that protects the confidentiality and security of the personal data involved.
