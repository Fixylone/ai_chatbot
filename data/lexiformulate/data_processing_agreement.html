Data Processing Agreement

Tool: LexiFormulate

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the Data Controller and the Data Processor to establish the terms and conditions under which LexiFormulate will automate the drafting of compliance-related legal documents based on industry standards and regulatory requirements.

LexiFormulate is a LegalTech tool designed to support legal teams in regulated industries such as finance, healthcare, and telecommunications by providing automated generation of compliance documents.

This Agreement outlines the roles, responsibilities, and obligations of the parties regarding the processing of personal data to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws.

1.1 Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the scope and conditions under which the Data Processor shall process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller using the LexiFormulate tool.

This Agreement applies to all processing activities undertaken by the Data Processor that involve personal data provided or collected by the Data Controller in connection with their use of LexiFormulate.

Both parties agree to comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations governing the processing of personal data within the scope of this Agreement.

1.2 Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

  • Data Controller: The natural or legal person who determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.
  • Data Processor: The natural or legal person who processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller using the LexiFormulate tool.
  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing: Any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, alignment, restriction, erasure, or destruction.
  • Data Subject: The identified or identifiable natural person to whom the personal data relates.
  • Sub-processor: Any third party engaged by the Data Processor to process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.

1.3 Interpretation and Construction

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, words denoting the singular include the plural and vice versa, and words denoting any gender include all genders.

Headings are included for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

References to clauses, sections, or schedules are to those in this Agreement unless otherwise specified.

The terms defined in Section 1.2 shall have the same meaning throughout this Agreement.

Any reference to a statutory provision includes any modification or re-enactment thereof for the time being in force.

2. Roles and Responsibilities

This section outlines the respective roles and responsibilities of the Data Controller and the Data Processor in relation to the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

The Data Controller retains overall responsibility for compliance with applicable data protection laws and for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data.

The Data Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Data Controller and shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect such data.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that the processing activities comply with this Agreement and all relevant legal requirements.

2.1 Data Controller and Data Processor

The Data Controller is the entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

The Data Processor is the entity that processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller by utilizing the LexiFormulate tool in accordance with documented instructions.

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor acknowledge their distinct roles under this Agreement and agree to fulfill their respective obligations to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws.

2.2 Responsibilities of the Data Processor

The Data Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Data Controller and strictly within the scope of this Agreement and the use of the LexiFormulate tool.

The Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security, confidentiality, and integrity of personal data processed on behalf of the Data Controller.

The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive adequate training regarding data protection requirements.

The Data Processor shall promptly notify the Data Controller of any data breaches or security incidents affecting personal data processed under this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in responding to data subject requests and in complying with applicable data protection laws, including cooperation with supervisory authorities where required.

The Data Processor shall maintain records of processing activities and provide such records or information to the Data Controller upon request to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

2.3 Responsibilities of the Data Controller

The Data Controller shall ensure that all personal data provided to the Data Processor through the use of LexiFormulate is accurate, complete, and collected in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

The Data Controller shall provide clear and documented instructions to the Data Processor regarding the processing of personal data, including the scope, purpose, and duration of processing activities.

The Data Controller shall ensure that appropriate legal bases for processing personal data are established and maintained in accordance with applicable regulations.

The Data Controller shall cooperate with the Data Processor to facilitate compliance with data subject rights and regulatory requirements, including responding to data subject requests and audits.

The Data Controller shall be responsible for obtaining any necessary consents or authorizations from data subjects for the processing of their personal data using LexiFormulate.

The Data Controller shall promptly notify the Data Processor of any changes to the processing instructions or any incidents that may impact the processing of personal data under this Agreement.

3. Data Processing Details

This section provides detailed information regarding the subject matter, duration, types of data, and locations involved in the processing activities carried out using the LexiFormulate tool.

The Data Processor shall process personal data strictly in accordance with the documented instructions provided by the Data Controller and within the scope defined in this Agreement.

All processing activities shall be conducted in compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations to ensure the security and confidentiality of personal data.

3.1 Subject Matter and Nature of Processing

The subject matter of the processing under this Agreement encompasses all personal data provided by the Data Controller to the Data Processor for the purpose of generating compliance-related legal documents using the LexiFormulate tool.

The nature of the processing includes collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, alignment, restriction, erasure, and destruction of personal data as directed by the Data Controller.

All processing activities shall be performed solely for the purposes specified by the Data Controller and in accordance with the documented instructions, ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws.

3.2 Duration of Processing

The processing of personal data by the Data Processor using LexiFormulate shall commence upon receipt of the data from the Data Controller and shall continue for the duration specified in this Agreement or until the termination of this Agreement, whichever occurs first.

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall cease processing personal data and shall, at the choice of the Data Controller, return or securely delete all personal data processed under this Agreement in accordance with Section 8.2.

The duration of processing may be extended only by mutual written agreement of the Data Controller and the Data Processor, subject to compliance with applicable data protection laws.

3.3 Types of Data and Data Categories

The types of personal data processed using LexiFormulate include, but are not limited to, identification data, contact information, employment details, and any other data categories necessary for the generation of compliance-related legal documents.

Data categories may include sensitive personal data only to the extent explicitly instructed by the Data Controller and permitted under applicable data protection laws.

The Data Processor shall process only those categories of personal data that are necessary for the performance of its obligations under this Agreement and shall not process personal data outside the documented instructions of the Data Controller.

Any changes to the types or categories of data processed shall be subject to prior written agreement between the Data Controller and the Data Processor.

3.4 Data Processing Locations

All processing of personal data using LexiFormulate shall take place within the jurisdictions agreed upon by the Data Controller and the Data Processor as specified in this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall ensure that any transfer of personal data to locations outside the agreed jurisdictions complies fully with applicable data protection laws and regulations, including the implementation of appropriate safeguards.

The Data Processor shall not process or store personal data in any location not authorized in writing by the Data Controller.

Any changes to the data processing locations shall require prior written consent from the Data Controller and shall be subject to compliance with all relevant legal requirements.

4. Data Security and Confidentiality

The Data Processor shall implement security measures that are reasonably appropriate to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Such security measures shall include, but are not limited to, access controls, encryption, physical security, and regular security assessments.

The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel involved in the processing of personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate training on data protection and security practices.

Both parties shall cooperate to promptly address any security incidents or breaches affecting personal data processed under this Agreement and shall notify each other in accordance with applicable laws.

The confidentiality obligations set forth in this section shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

4.1 Security Measures

The Data Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk associated with the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

These measures shall include, but are not limited to, encryption of personal data both at rest and in transit, access controls to restrict data processing to authorized personnel, and regular testing and evaluation of the effectiveness of security measures.

The Data Processor shall ensure that all systems and software used in connection with LexiFormulate are kept up-to-date with security patches and that vulnerabilities are promptly addressed.

Physical security controls shall be implemented to protect facilities and hardware where personal data is processed or stored.

The Data Processor shall maintain incident response procedures to detect, report, and respond to security breaches in a timely manner, and shall cooperate with the Data Controller in managing any such incidents.

4.2 Confidentiality Obligations

The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to process personal data using LexiFormulate are subject to strict confidentiality obligations, whether arising by law, contract, or professional duty.

Confidentiality obligations shall extend beyond the term of this Agreement and shall survive its termination or expiration.

The Data Processor shall implement appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized disclosure or access to personal data, including confidentiality agreements and access restrictions.

Any disclosure of personal data to third parties shall be subject to prior written approval from the Data Controller, except as required by law, in which case the Data Processor shall notify the Data Controller promptly unless prohibited by law.

The Data Processor shall cooperate with the Data Controller to ensure ongoing compliance with confidentiality obligations and to address any potential breaches or concerns.

5. Sub-processing and Data Transfers

This section governs the conditions under which sub-processing may be engaged and the terms applicable to international data transfers involving personal data processed using LexiFormulate.

The Data Processor shall not subcontract any processing of personal data without prior written authorization from the Data Controller, except as permitted under this Agreement.

Any engagement of Sub-processors shall be subject to a written contract imposing data protection obligations no less stringent than those set forth in this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall remain fully liable to the Data Controller for the performance of any Sub-processor's obligations.

International transfers of personal data processed by the Data Processor using LexiFormulate shall comply with applicable data protection laws and shall only be conducted where appropriate safeguards are in place.

The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with sufficient information regarding the location of data processing and any transfers to enable the Data Controller to comply with its legal obligations.

Any changes to Sub-processors or data transfer locations shall require prior written consent from the Data Controller and shall be subject to compliance with all relevant legal requirements.

5.1 Sub-processing Conditions

The Data Processor shall not engage any Sub-processor to carry out processing activities involving personal data without the prior written consent of the Data Controller.

Where consent is granted, the Data Processor shall ensure that the Sub-processor is bound by data protection obligations that are no less stringent than those set forth in this Agreement and that such obligations are documented in a written contract.

The Data Processor shall remain fully liable to the Data Controller for the performance of any Sub-processor's obligations and for ensuring compliance with this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with information about any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Sub-processors, allowing the Data Controller the opportunity to object to such changes within a reasonable period.

The Data Processor shall monitor the activities of Sub-processors to ensure ongoing compliance with applicable data protection laws and the terms of this Agreement.

Any engagement of Sub-processors shall be subject to the same security, confidentiality, and data protection requirements as those applicable to the Data Processor under this Agreement and in connection with the use of LexiFormulate.

5.2 International Data Transfers

The Data Processor shall ensure that any international transfer of personal data processed using LexiFormulate complies fully with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Such transfers shall only occur to countries or territories that provide an adequate level of data protection as determined by the relevant supervisory authorities or through appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses or binding corporate rules.

The Data Processor shall obtain prior written authorization from the Data Controller before initiating any international data transfer and shall provide sufficient information to enable the Data Controller to comply with its legal obligations.

Where required by applicable law, the Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in ensuring that data subjects' rights are protected in the context of international data transfers.

The Data Processor shall maintain records of all international data transfers and shall promptly notify the Data Controller of any changes affecting such transfers.

6. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects

Data Subjects whose personal data is processed using LexiFormulate have rights under applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing.

The Data Controller shall inform Data Subjects of their rights and the means by which they may exercise such rights in relation to the processing of their personal data using LexiFormulate.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in facilitating the exercise of Data Subject rights by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures to respond to requests in a timely and compliant manner.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that data subject requests are handled efficiently and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

6.1 Data Subject Rights

Data Subjects have the right to access their personal data processed using LexiFormulate and to obtain information regarding the processing activities.

Data Subjects may request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, as well as erasure of personal data where applicable under data protection laws.

Data Subjects are entitled to request restriction of processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and exercise their right to data portability where applicable.

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in fulfilling these rights by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures to handle Data Subject requests promptly and in compliance with applicable laws.

The Data Controller shall ensure that Data Subjects are informed of their rights and the procedures for exercising them in relation to the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

6.2 Assistance to Data Controller

The Data Processor shall assist the Data Controller in responding to data subject requests, but shall not be obligated to provide any support related to data portability requests.

Such assistance shall include the provision of necessary information and support to enable the Data Controller to comply with obligations under applicable data protection laws.

The Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to facilitate the timely and effective handling of data subject requests in coordination with the Data Controller.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that any requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection to processing are addressed in accordance with legal requirements.

Where required, the Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with relevant documentation and evidence of compliance to support audits or regulatory inquiries.

This section outlines the rights of the Data Controller to audit the Data Processor's compliance with this Agreement and the obligations of both parties to ensure ongoing adherence to applicable data protection laws.

The Data Controller shall have the right to conduct audits, including inspections and reviews, of the Data Processor's facilities, systems, and procedures related to the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and upon reasonable prior notice, and shall be carried out in a manner that minimizes disruption to the Data Processor's operations.

The Data Processor shall provide all necessary assistance and access to information to enable the Data Controller to verify compliance with this Agreement and applicable data protection requirements.

Both parties shall cooperate in addressing any findings or recommendations resulting from audits and shall implement corrective actions as necessary.

The Data Processor shall maintain documentation and records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and shall make such records available to the Data Controller upon request.

Confidentiality obligations shall apply to all information obtained during audits, and such information shall be used solely for the purpose of verifying compliance with this Agreement.

7.1 Audit Rights

The Data Controller shall have the right to conduct audits, including inspections and reviews, of the Data Processor's facilities, systems, and procedures related to the processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and upon reasonable prior notice to the Data Processor to minimize operational disruption.

The Data Processor shall provide full cooperation and grant access to all relevant information, documentation, and personnel necessary for the Data Controller to verify compliance with this Agreement and applicable data protection laws.

Audit findings shall be documented and shared with the Data Processor, who shall promptly address any identified deficiencies or non-compliance issues.

Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality regarding audit information and results, using such information solely for compliance verification purposes.

7.2 Compliance Monitoring

Both the Data Controller and the Data Processor shall establish and maintain ongoing compliance monitoring procedures to ensure adherence to the terms of this Agreement and applicable data protection laws in connection with the use of LexiFormulate.

The Data Processor shall regularly review and update its policies, technical measures, and organizational practices to address evolving regulatory requirements and emerging risks related to personal data processing.

The Data Controller shall monitor the Data Processor's compliance through periodic assessments and shall promptly address any identified issues or non-compliance.

Both parties shall cooperate in the implementation of corrective actions and continuous improvement measures to maintain a high standard of data protection and security.

Documentation of compliance monitoring activities shall be maintained and made available to the other party upon reasonable request to demonstrate ongoing adherence to this Agreement.

8. Termination and Data Return or Deletion

This section sets forth the procedures and obligations of the parties upon termination or expiration of this Agreement concerning the return or deletion of personal data processed using LexiFormulate.

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall, at the choice of the Data Controller, either return all personal data processed under this Agreement or securely delete such data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

The Data Processor shall certify to the Data Controller that all personal data has been returned or deleted, except for any data that must be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Any retention of personal data beyond the termination or expiration of this Agreement shall be limited to the minimum necessary period and purpose, and the Data Processor shall continue to apply appropriate security and confidentiality measures during such retention.

Both parties shall cooperate to ensure that the termination procedures are conducted in a manner that protects the rights and interests of Data Subjects and complies with all regulatory requirements.

8.1 Termination Procedures

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall immediately cease all processing of personal data using LexiFormulate.

The Data Processor shall cooperate fully with the Data Controller to ensure an orderly and secure transition or termination of data processing activities.

All instructions regarding the return or deletion of personal data shall be followed promptly and in accordance with the Data Controller's documented directives and applicable data protection laws.

The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with written confirmation of the completion of all termination procedures, including the return or secure deletion of personal data, within a reasonable time frame.

Any ongoing obligations related to confidentiality, data security, and compliance shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

8.2 Data Return and Deletion

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall, at the choice of the Data Controller, return all personal data processed under this Agreement or securely delete such data in a manner that prevents its recovery.

The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with written certification confirming that all personal data has been returned or deleted, except for any data that must be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Any personal data retained pursuant to legal obligations shall be subject to continued confidentiality and security measures as required under this Agreement.

The Data Processor shall cooperate with the Data Controller to ensure that all data return or deletion activities are completed promptly and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Both parties shall document the data return or deletion process to demonstrate compliance with their respective obligations under this Agreement.
