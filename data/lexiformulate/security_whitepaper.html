2.1 Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) Framework

The CIA framework is a foundational model for information security that LexiFormulate incorporates to ensure comprehensive protection of data and systems.

Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals and processes, preventing unauthorized disclosure. LexiFormulate automates controls such as encryption, access restrictions, and data classification to uphold confidentiality.

Integrity guarantees that information remains accurate, complete, and unaltered except by authorized actions. Through automated validation checks, audit trails, and change management procedures, LexiFormulate supports maintaining data integrity.

Availability ensures that authorized users have reliable and timely access to information and resources when needed. LexiFormulate facilitates availability by automating redundancy, backup, and recovery controls aligned with industry best practices.

Together, these three principles form the cornerstone of the security architecture and controls that LexiFormulate helps organizations implement, monitor, and document for compliance and operational resilience.

Access control mechanisms are critical components within the security architecture automated by LexiFormulate to regulate and restrict user access to information systems and data based on defined permissions and roles.
