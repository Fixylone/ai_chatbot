7.1 List of Applicable Standards and Regulations

This appendix provides a comprehensive list of the key standards and regulations applicable to organizations utilizing LexiFormulate for compliance and certification management across regulated industries such as finance, healthcare, and telecommunications.

  • ISO/IEC 27001: International standard for information security management systems (ISMS), providing requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an ISMS.
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework: A voluntary framework consisting of standards, guidelines, and best practices to manage cybersecurity-related risk.
  • HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, setting standards for protecting sensitive patient health information in the healthcare sector.
  • PCI DSS: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard, a set of security standards designed to ensure that all companies that accept, process, store or transmit credit card information maintain a secure environment.
  • GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation, a regulation in EU law on data protection and privacy in the European Union and the European Economic Area.
  • SOX: Sarbanes-Oxley Act, establishing requirements for financial recordkeeping and reporting for publicly traded companies.
  • FISMA: Federal Information Security Management Act, requiring federal agencies to develop, document, and implement information security programs.
  • CCPA: California Consumer Privacy Act, enhancing privacy rights and consumer protection for residents of California.

LexiFormulate incorporates these standards and regulations into its automated compliance documentation framework to assist organizations in maintaining alignment with current legal and industry requirements.

Organizations are advised to consult the specific text of these standards and regulations, as well as any jurisdiction-specific amendments or additional requirements, to ensure full compliance within their operational contexts.

7.2 Glossary of Terms

This glossary provides definitions of key terms used throughout this compliance and certification framework automated by LexiFormulate, ensuring consistent understanding and interpretation.

Audit Trail
A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities affecting a specific operation, procedure, or event related to compliance management.
Certification Body
An accredited organization authorized to assess and issue certifications confirming compliance with specified standards and regulatory requirements.
Compliance
The state of conforming to all applicable laws, regulations, standards, and contractual obligations relevant to the organization's operations.
Corrective Action
A measure taken to eliminate the causes of a detected non-compliance or deficiency to prevent recurrence.
Incident Reporting
The process of documenting and communicating any events that may adversely affect compliance status or data security.
LexiFormulate
The software tool designed to automate the drafting of compliance-related legal documents based on industry standards and regulatory requirements.
Non-Compliance
Failure to adhere to applicable laws, regulations, standards, or contractual obligations.
Regulatory Framework
The collection of laws, regulations, standards, and guidelines governing compliance obligations within a specific industry or jurisdiction.
Risk Management
The systematic identification, evaluation, and mitigation of risks that could impact compliance objectives.
Verification
The process of confirming that compliance activities and controls meet defined requirements and standards.

Users of LexiFormulate are encouraged to refer to this glossary to ensure clarity and uniformity in the interpretation of terms across compliance documentation and communications.
