2.1 Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) Model

The Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) model constitutes the foundational security principles that guide enterprise data protection efforts supported by LegalForge.

Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized personnel, preventing unauthorized disclosure through technical and administrative controls.

Integrity guarantees that data remains accurate, complete, and unaltered except by authorized actions, thereby maintaining trustworthiness throughout its lifecycle.

Availability assures that authorized users have reliable and timely access to information and resources as needed to perform their functions.

LegalForge incorporates these principles into its automated documentation processes, enabling legal teams and compliance officers to establish and enforce security policies that uphold the CIA triad effectively.

Network security measures constitute a fundamental layer of defense within the Security Architecture, designed to protect enterprise data from unauthorized access and cyber threats.

Data encryption protocols are essential technical security controls that safeguard enterprise data both at rest and in transit within environments supported by LegalForge.

LegalForge assists legal teams and compliance officers in documenting the implementation of robust encryption standards such as AES-256 for data at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher for data in transit.

Access control mechanisms are vital technical security controls within the Security Architecture framework that regulate and restrict access to enterprise data and systems based on predefined policies.

LegalForge supports legal teams and compliance officers in documenting these mechanisms, which include role-based access control (RBAC), mandatory access control (MAC), and discretionary access control (DAC) models tailored to organizational requirements.

These mechanisms enforce the principle of least privilege, ensuring users and processes are granted only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their functions.

Additional controls include multi-factor authentication (MFA), session management, and regular access reviews to detect and remediate unauthorized or excessive permissions.

By integrating access control mechanisms into automated documentation, LegalForge enables organizations to maintain consistent enforcement of security policies and demonstrate compliance with applicable regulatory standards.
