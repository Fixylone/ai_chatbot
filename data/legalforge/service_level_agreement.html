1. Introduction

This Service Level Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Customer and the Provider to define the terms and conditions under which the Provider will deliver services using LegalForge. LegalForge automates the creation of compliance-focused legal documentation for enterprise data handling practices, ensuring adherence to applicable regulatory requirements.

This Agreement sets forth the mutual understanding of the parties regarding the scope, performance standards, responsibilities, and other essential provisions governing the use of LegalForge in connection with the services provided.

1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish clear and enforceable terms under which the Provider will deliver services utilizing LegalForge, thereby ensuring consistent compliance with applicable laws and regulations governing data handling and processing.

This Agreement aims to define the rights and obligations of both the Provider and the Customer in relation to service delivery, performance expectations, data security, and risk management, thereby fostering a transparent and accountable operational relationship.

1.2 Scope of Services

This Agreement covers the provision of services by the Provider using LegalForge, which includes the automation of compliance-focused legal documentation for enterprise data handling practices.

The services encompass document generation, customization, and maintenance support to ensure that legal documents remain up-to-date with evolving regulatory requirements.

Excluded from this scope are any consulting services beyond the standard functionality of LegalForge, as well as any third-party integrations or custom software development unless expressly agreed upon in writing by both parties.

The Provider shall deliver services utilizing LegalForge to automate the creation of compliance-focused legal documentation tailored for enterprise data handling practices.

These services include document template generation, customization based on Customer requirements, and ongoing updates to ensure compliance with evolving legal and regulatory standards.

LegalForge enables the Provider to efficiently produce accurate and consistent legal documents, thereby supporting the Customer's compliance obligations under applicable laws.

2.2 Service Levels and Performance Metrics

The Provider commits to maintaining defined service levels to ensure the effective operation of LegalForge and the timely delivery of compliance documentation services.

Key performance metrics include system availability, response times for support requests, accuracy rates of generated documents, and the timeliness of updates reflecting regulatory changes.

System availability shall be measured monthly, with the Provider targeting a minimum uptime of 99.9% to ensure continuous access to LegalForge.

Response times for support inquiries will be categorized by severity levels, with critical issues receiving initial response within one hour and resolution within four hours wherever feasible.

Accuracy of documentation generated by LegalForge shall be regularly audited to maintain compliance with applicable laws and standards, with error rates not exceeding 0.5% of total documents produced.

The Provider guarantees 100% uptime for LegalForge, but scheduled maintenance windows may occur without prior notice, which can affect availability.

Availability metrics will be tracked monthly, and the Provider will report on uptime performance as part of the regular service reports delivered to the Customer.

2.2.2 Response and Resolution Times

The Provider shall respond to all Customer inquiries and support requests according to the severity level assigned to each issue.

Critical issues impacting the availability or core functionality of LegalForge will receive an initial response within one hour and a resolution target of four hours, subject to the complexity of the issue.

High-priority issues, which affect significant features but do not cause complete service outages, will receive an initial response within four hours and a resolution target of one business day.

Medium and low-priority issues, including minor bugs or general questions, will receive a response within one business day and a resolution timeframe agreed upon with the Customer based on issue complexity.

The Provider shall keep the Customer informed of progress during the resolution process through regular updates until the issue is resolved or an acceptable workaround is implemented.

Response and resolution times are measured from the time a support request is received by the Provider through the designated communication channels.

2.2.3 Reporting and Monitoring

The Provider shall provide reports on system performance and compliance metrics at regular intervals, which may vary depending on operational needs and Customer requests.
