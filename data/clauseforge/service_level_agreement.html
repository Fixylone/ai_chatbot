Service Level Agreement

Tool: ClauseForge

1. Introduction

This Service Level Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the Customer and the Provider. It sets forth the terms and conditions under which the Provider will deliver services to the Customer using ClauseForge, a tool that automates the drafting of contractual clauses for legal compliance.

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the service levels, responsibilities, and commitments of each party to ensure a clear understanding and effective management of the services provided.

1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish clear and measurable service level commitments between the Provider and the Customer, ensuring that the services delivered using ClauseForge meet agreed-upon standards of quality, availability, and performance.

This Agreement aims to facilitate effective communication, accountability, and resolution processes to support the successful delivery and management of services throughout the term of engagement.

1.2 Scope of Services

This Agreement covers the provision of services by the Provider utilizing ClauseForge to automate the drafting of contractual clauses for legal compliance. The services include the generation, review, and delivery of legally compliant contractual clauses tailored to the Customer's requirements.

The scope encompasses all related support, maintenance, and updates necessary to ensure the continuous and effective operation of ClauseForge as part of the service delivery.

Any additional services outside the automated drafting and related support shall be subject to separate agreements between the parties.

1.3 Definitions and Interpretations

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

  • "Agreement" means this Service Level Agreement including all its terms, conditions, and schedules as amended from time to time.
  • "ClauseForge" refers to the software tool used by the Provider to automate the drafting of contractual clauses for legal compliance.
  • "Customer" means the party receiving the services under this Agreement.
  • "Provider" means the party providing the services described herein using ClauseForge.
  • "Service Levels" means the standards of service performance and availability as set forth in this Agreement.
  • "Incident" means any event which is not part of the standard operation of the service and which causes, or may cause, an interruption to or a reduction in the quality of that service.

Interpretations shall be made in accordance with the rules of construction applicable to contracts, with headings for convenience only and without affecting the interpretation.

2. Service Description and Performance Standards

This section outlines the services provided by the Provider using ClauseForge and the performance standards to which these services will adhere. The Provider commits to delivering automated contractual clause drafting services that meet the quality, availability, and responsiveness requirements specified herein.

The services include the generation, review, and delivery of legally compliant contractual clauses tailored to the Customer's needs, leveraging ClauseForge's automation capabilities to enhance efficiency and accuracy.

Performance standards are established to ensure that the services maintain a high level of reliability and meet agreed service levels, as further detailed in subsequent sections of this Agreement.

2.1 Service Description

The Provider shall deliver services utilizing ClauseForge to automate the drafting of contractual clauses for legal compliance. This includes the generation, customization, and provision of contractual clauses that meet the Customer's specified requirements.

The service encompasses continuous updates and maintenance of ClauseForge to ensure that the automated drafting reflects current legal standards and regulatory requirements.

Additionally, the Provider will offer support to address any technical issues or questions related to the use of ClauseForge as part of the service delivery.

2.2 Service Levels and Performance Metrics

The Provider commits to maintaining service levels that ensure the reliable and efficient operation of ClauseForge. The key service levels include availability, response time, and accuracy of the contractual clauses generated.

Performance metrics will be measured and reported regularly to assess compliance with the agreed service levels. These metrics include:

  • Availability: The percentage of time ClauseForge is operational and accessible to the Customer, measured monthly.
  • Response Time: The time taken to respond to Customer inquiries or incidents related to ClauseForge.
  • Accuracy: The degree to which the generated contractual clauses meet the Customer's specified requirements and comply with applicable legal standards.

The Provider will use industry-standard monitoring tools and techniques to track these metrics and provide transparent reporting to the Customer as stipulated in Section 4.1.

2.2.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The Provider shall monitor and report on the following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to ensure adherence to the service levels defined in this Agreement:

  • System Uptime: The percentage of time ClauseForge is fully operational and accessible during each calendar month, with a target of 99.9% availability.
  • Incident Response Time: The average time taken to acknowledge and respond to Customer-reported incidents related to ClauseForge.
  • Incident Resolution Time: The average time required to resolve incidents and restore normal service operation.
  • Accuracy Rate: The percentage of contractual clauses generated by ClauseForge that meet the Customer's specifications without requiring manual correction.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Measured through periodic surveys assessing the Customer's satisfaction with the services provided using ClauseForge.

These KPIs will be reviewed regularly in accordance with Section 4.3 to facilitate continuous service improvement and ensure the Provider meets its commitments under this Agreement.

2.2.2 Service Availability and Uptime

The Provider guarantees that ClauseForge will achieve a minimum service availability of 99.9% during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance periods.

Service availability is defined as the total time ClauseForge is operational and accessible to the Customer, measured using automated monitoring tools and verified through incident reports.

Scheduled maintenance windows will be communicated to the Customer at least 72 hours in advance and will be planned to minimize disruption.

In the event that availability falls below the guaranteed threshold, the provisions for service credits and remedies as outlined in Section 5.1 will apply.

2.2.3 Response and Resolution Times

The Provider commits to timely response and resolution of incidents affecting the operation of ClauseForge to ensure minimal disruption to the Customer's use of the services.

  • Response Time: The Provider shall acknowledge and respond to Customer-reported incidents within 1 hour during business hours and within 4 hours outside business hours.
  • Resolution Time: The Provider aims to resolve incidents based on their severity level, with critical incidents resolved within 4 hours, high severity incidents within 8 hours, and medium or low severity incidents within 24 to 72 hours.

These response and resolution times will be monitored and reported as part of the service performance metrics to ensure compliance with the agreed service levels.

Should the Provider be unable to meet these targets due to circumstances beyond their control, the Customer will be notified promptly with an estimated time for resolution.

2.3 Service Level Targets and Commitments

The Provider commits to achieving the following service level targets in relation to the operation and delivery of services using ClauseForge:

  • Service Availability: Maintain a minimum uptime of 99.9% per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance as defined in Section 2.2.2.
  • Incident Response: Respond to all Customer-reported incidents within the timeframes specified in Section 2.2.3.
  • Incident Resolution: Resolve incidents in accordance with the severity-based resolution times outlined in Section 2.2.3.
  • Accuracy: Ensure that at least 98% of contractual clauses generated by ClauseForge meet the Customer’s specifications without requiring manual correction.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Achieve an average customer satisfaction score of 4 out of 5 or higher in periodic surveys conducted as part of service monitoring.

The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet or exceed these targets and will report performance against these commitments as part of the regular service reviews described in Section 4.3.

This section describes the processes and responsibilities related to the ongoing management, monitoring, and reporting of services delivered through ClauseForge.

4.1 Service Monitoring and Reporting

The Provider shall implement continuous monitoring of ClauseForge to ensure adherence to the service levels and performance metrics established in this Agreement.

4.2 Incident Management and Escalation Procedures

The Provider shall establish and maintain a structured incident management process to promptly address and resolve any incidents affecting the operation of ClauseForge.

Upon identification or notification of an incident, the Provider will log and classify the incident according to its severity to prioritize response and resolution efforts effectively.

  • Incident Logging: All incidents reported by the Customer or detected by monitoring tools will be recorded with relevant details including time, nature, and impact.
  • Incident Classification: Incidents will be categorized as Critical, High, Medium, or Low severity based on their impact on service availability and Customer operations.
  • Incident Response: The Provider will initiate response activities in accordance with the severity level, aiming to acknowledge and begin resolution within the response times specified in Section 2.2.3.
  • Escalation Procedures: If an incident cannot be resolved within the target resolution time, it will be escalated to higher levels of technical and management personnel to ensure timely resolution.

The Provider will keep the Customer informed of incident status updates, including estimated resolution times and any interim workarounds available.

Following incident resolution, the Provider will conduct a root cause analysis and implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence, sharing relevant findings with the Customer as appropriate.

These incident management and escalation procedures are designed to minimize service disruption and maintain the reliability of ClauseForge as part of the service delivery commitments under this Agreement.

5. Compensation and Penalties

This section sets forth the compensation mechanisms and penalties applicable in the event of the Provider's failure to meet the service levels specified in this Agreement.

In the event that the Provider does not meet the agreed service levels, the Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits or other remedies as specified herein.

Penalties for failure to meet the agreed Service Level Objectives (SLOs) will be applied as described in Section 5.2.

Both parties agree to cooperate in the calculation and application of any compensation to ensure fair and transparent resolution of service level breaches.

5.1 Service Credits and Remedies

Service credits shall be applied automatically to the Customer's account within 30 days following the verification of a service level breach .

The amount of service credits will be calculated based on the severity and duration of the breach, in accordance with the following schedule:

  • For each 0.1% below the 99.9% availability target, a credit equal to 5% of the monthly service fee will be granted.
  • For failure to meet incident response or resolution times, credits will be proportional to the delay beyond the agreed thresholds.

Service credits represent the sole and exclusive remedy for the Customer in the event of service level failures, except as otherwise expressly provided in this Agreement.

The Customer must notify the Provider in writing within 15 days of receiving a service credit to dispute its calculation or applicability.

Nothing in this section shall limit the Customer's rights to terminate the Agreement as provided in Section 6.3 for repeated or severe service level breaches.

5.2 Penalties for Non-Performance

In the event that the Provider fails to meet the service levels and performance commitments set forth in this Agreement, penalties shall be applied as follows to incentivize compliance and compensate the Customer for service degradation.

  • For each occurrence of service availability falling below the guaranteed 99.9% threshold within a calendar month, the Provider shall incur a penalty equivalent to 10% of the monthly service fee for each 0.1% shortfall.
  • Failure to meet the incident response and resolution times as specified in Section 2.2.3 will result in penalties proportionate to the duration of delay beyond the agreed targets, calculated on a sliding scale up to a maximum of 20% of the monthly service fee per incident.
  • Repeated breaches of service levels within a rolling three-month period may result in escalated penalties, including but not limited to increased service credits, additional financial penalties, or termination rights as set forth in Section 6.3.

All penalties shall be calculated and applied in accordance with the procedures described in Section 5.1, and the Provider shall provide detailed documentation supporting the calculation upon request.

The application of penalties does not preclude the Customer from seeking other remedies available under this Agreement or applicable law.
