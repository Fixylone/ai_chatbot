1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of ClauseForge. This document outlines how ClauseForge collects, uses, and protects personal data in the course of providing its legal document automation services. 1.1 Purpose of This Privacy Policy

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to inform users of ClauseForge about the types of personal data collected, the methods of collection, and the ways in which such data is used, shared, and protected. 1.2 Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data processed by ClauseForge in connection with its provision of legal document automation services. The Policy applies to all users of ClauseForge, including legal teams, contract managers, and any other individuals or entities interacting with ClauseForge's services. It governs the collection, use, sharing, and protection of personal data within the scope of ClauseForge's operations and is designed to comply with relevant data protection laws and regulations applicable to ClauseForge's services globally.

In this Privacy Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context requires otherwise.
