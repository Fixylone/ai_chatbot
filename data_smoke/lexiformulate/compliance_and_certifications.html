Certification Process

The certification process under LexiFormulate’s framework involves a comprehensive series of steps designed to ensure that organizations meet the requisite standards for compliance and security. Initially, applicants must submit a detailed application along with all necessary documentation evidencing their current compliance status and operational practices. This documentation is subject to review by the certifying body to verify accuracy and completeness.

Following the application review, an audit procedure is conducted, which may include on-site assessments, interviews, and technical evaluations. The scope and depth of these audits depend on the certification standards being pursued. Organizations are expected to cooperate fully and provide access to relevant systems, records, and personnel during this process. Any deficiencies identified during the audit must be addressed through a remediation plan, which is subject to follow-up assessments by the certifying authority.

Once the organization successfully completes the audit and any necessary remediation, a certification decision is issued. This certification affirms that the organization complies with the applicable standards and is authorized to display the certification mark. It is important to note that ongoing compliance monitoring and periodic re-evaluation are integral to maintaining certification status, as detailed in subsequent sections of this document.

Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for any failure or delay in performance under this agreement due to events beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, riots, embargoes, natural disasters, or governmental actions. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly upon becoming aware of such an event and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact of such force majeure event.

In the event that a force majeure event persists for a period exceeding thirty (30) days, either party shall have the right to terminate this agreement upon written notice to the other party without penalty or further obligation. This provision is intended to allocate the risks arising from extraordinary events and to ensure the continuity of compliance obligations as far as reasonably possible under the circumstances.
